Click a bot, then click any node as its destination — the path is A*-computed and highlighted right away, but the bot won't move until you press its ▶ Play button in the sidebar, or the ▶ Play All button in the header to start every commanded bot at once. Press ⏸ Pause (per bot or all) at any time to freeze mid-route, then Resume to continue. All running bots move concurrently every tick and negotiate collisions by priority — a paused bot still counts as an obstacle for other bots' A* routing. Obstacles are checked in real time: placing one on any bot's route — even a highlighted-but-not-yet-started one, or one it's mid-way through — triggers an immediate reroute, and the affected bot posts a 🚧 note in its own card plus the shared log so the rest of the fleet effectively "knows" about the new blockage. When a collision (bot or obstacle) is flagged, the lower-priority bot automatically replans a brand-new A* route around it — no manual input needed; it only falls back to a one-node sidestep-and-return if literally no alternate route exists. Priority is fixed A>B>C>D with aging so no one waits forever.
